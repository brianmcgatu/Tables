How it works

You get two attempts. A correct first attempt advances immediately. A second mistake reveals the answer before moving on.

Precision timing
Question times use performance.now() rather than the system clock for stable high-resolution measurements.

Pass-and-play match

Players share one device and alternate after every completed question. Handoffs are never timed.

Player profiles

Profiles are loaded from the JSON structure in the JavaScript PLAYER_PROFILES constant. Each player receives their own visual accent and performance record.
